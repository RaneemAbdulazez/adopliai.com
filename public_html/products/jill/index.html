How Jill Works

From job site to invoice in three steps

Step 01

Arrive & Document

Mike opens Jill, sees today's jobs pulled from Jobber. He taps a job, starts the stopwatch, and snaps Before photos. All with one thumb.

  • Jobs list synced from Jobber
  • One-tap directions to site
  • Before / During / After photo tagging
  • Stopwatch for labour tracking

Step 02

Scan & Build

As Mike uses parts, he scans the barcode or picks from the price book. No typing. No memorising part numbers. Jill builds the invoice line by line.

  • Barcode scanning for instant lookup
  • Fuzzy price book search
  • Quantities adjust on duplicate scan
  • Manual entry fallback for unknown items

Step 03

Submit & Go

Job done. Mike hits Submit. Jill closes the visit, pushes the invoice to Jobber, uploads photos, and AI-polishes his voice notes into professional language.

  • Active or draft invoice to Jobber
  • AI rewrites voice notes professionally
  • AI flags likely missing items for admin
  • Automatic retry if offline

Features

Built for the field, not the office

Every feature passes one test: can a technician use it with one dirty thumb, standing in the sun, in under 10 seconds?

Barcode Scanner

Scan any manufacturer UPC. Jill resolves it to your company price book instantly. Duplicate scans increment quantity — no double entries.

Smart Photo Capture

Tag photos as Before, During, or After. Compressed client-side, uploaded in background. Never blocks the tech from working.

AI Voice Notes

Hold to record. On submit, AI rewrites your voice notes in professional, client-facing language. Multiple notes merged into one clean summary.

Labour Stopwatch

Start, pause, resume. Timer persists through app kills and phone locks. Duration pushed to Jobber as a time entry automatically.

Offline-First

Jobs, price book, and line items all work without connectivity. Submit offline — it queues and auto-retries when you're back online.

AI Missing Item Detection

On submit, AI analyses the job and flags likely missing items. Flags appear as internal notes in Jobber for your admin to review.

Three-Way Close

Submit Invoice (live to client), Invoice Later (draft for admin), or Save & Exit (keep working). Full flexibility for every situation.

Enterprise Security

Multi-tenant isolation, encrypted tokens, signed URLs with 1-hour expiry. Your data is yours — no company can see another's.

Jobber Integration

Bi-directional sync via Jobber's GraphQL API. Jobs, invoices, photos, time entries, and notes — all pushed to the right place.
